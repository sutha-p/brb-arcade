BRB Arcade

Quick game breaks that end on time. Pick 2, 5, 10 or 15 minutes, play a game that fits, and get sent back to the tab you left when time is up.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store Support BRB Arcade

How breaks work

  1. Pick a length

    Click the BRB Arcade icon and choose 2, 5, 10 or 15 minutes. The clock starts right away.

  2. Play

    A Break tab opens with games that fit the time you have. Reminders tell you how long is left.

  3. Back on time

    When time's up, one click takes you back to the tab you came from.

Privacy first

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. BRB Arcade cannot read the pages you visit. Your settings and game progress stay in Chrome on your own computer (settings can follow you through your own Chrome sync). This website has no trackers either.
